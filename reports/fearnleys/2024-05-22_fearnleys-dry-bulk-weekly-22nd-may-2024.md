## Capesize/Newcastlemax 

The count of laden vessels heading to the Far East currently located either in the Atlantic or Indian Ocean have increased notably the last month. 
The count was similar in mid-April, mid-January and through most of Q4 last year. Market levels should therefore continue to be strongly supported in the high teens going forward. 

Imported iron ore consumption in China continues to rise, and basis the usual lead this has on the Capesize market one could expect a rally to materialize in June. The iron ore price lead suggests the market will bottom in June, and rally into July.

## Panamax/Kamsarmax 

Bullish development in AIS data the last two weeks, as the count of vessels heading to or in the South Atlantic have risen to 420 vessels. 
The below chart shows the BPI82 index against the count of vessels heading to or in the South Atlantic exceeding 375 vessels. The last four years, the market has rallied each of the five times following this development. The rallies have ranged from +33% to +100%, and it took between 2 and 4 weeks for each of the rallies to materialize. 

The above is supported by macro indicators and average market seasonality. So, it looks like the third quarter is likely to average higher than the second.

## Supramax/Ultramax 

In view of the outlook for the larger segments, average market seasonality, leading macro indicators and strong fundamental data, it is hard to expect anything else than year-to-date highs in the third quarter. Seasonality suggest the market might continue to bump around within a 15.000 - 16.000 USD per day range the next two months before another rally. However, we think a rally will materialize within the next month.
