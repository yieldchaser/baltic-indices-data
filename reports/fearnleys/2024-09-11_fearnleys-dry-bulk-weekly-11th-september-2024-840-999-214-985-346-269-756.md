## Capesize/Newcastlemax 

Like last week, we see bullish and bearish factors, making it hard to have a clear opinion on the near-term market direction. 
**Bearish factors:**
- The Capesize / Kamsarmax ratio. 
- Imported iron ore consumption in China. 
- Iron ore and steel price leads. 
- The seasonal peak in Brazilian iron ore shipments is behind us. 
**Bullish factors:** 
- High count of VLOCs heading to Brazil. 
- Bauxite shipments have started to increase. 
- Coal demand could increase in Q4. 
- Forecasts by most steel/iron ore analysts are for continued inventory building in China.

## Panamax/Kamsarmax 

This segment getting help from both the smaller and bigger sizes now. 
The Kamsarmax index is significantly lower than both the Supramax and Capesize index. 

The US grain shipment season is underway, with many more vessels to be absorbed there before the seasonal peak. 
Coal market reports are all painting a bearish picture, however. Demand should nonetheless see a seasonal uptick in Q4.

## Supramax/Ultramax 

Last week, we wrote the following:

*Direction-wise, average seasonality continued to be tracked closely, although market levels have been lower than what seasonality suggested. 
Should seasonality continue to be tracked, there will be another bounce next week.*

Based on today's FFA movement the market will bounce from current levels. A range of macro indicators looks supportive through the remainder of this year, meaning that last year's Oct-Dec market average is likely to be higher this year.
