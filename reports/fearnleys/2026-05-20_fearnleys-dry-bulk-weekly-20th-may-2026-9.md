## Capesize/Newcastlemax

Two weeks ago, we wrote *"The market has kept rallying, diverging from the development of the laden/ballast ratio and the South Atlantic tightness indicator. The C5TC 180/P5TC ratio has usually peaked around 2.5 in the last years, which suggests an index of around 50kpd might be the ceiling for the current rally."*

We expect the market to continue correcting in the near-term. 

**Top left chart:** Iron ore consumption has been unchanged for several weeks. Increasing mill profitability suggest consumption could rise in the near-term. However, the peak season is here now, so upside potential is probably limited. 
**Top right chart:** Shipment volumes were 6.5% higher than last year through week 20. Still strongly positive, but down from the peak earlier this year. Supply growth is at 1.5%. 
**Bottom left chart:** The SATI is still low, suggesting the market remains in toppish territory. 
**Bottom right chart:** The laden/ballast ratio have turned down sharply over the last weeks. The reading is now close to the long-term average of 1.05, and is thus not suggesting anything clear in either direction.

## Handysize

Two weeks ago, we wrote *"Further signs of a market peak since last week. The Ultramax rally has stalled, and the laden/ballast ratio have turned down (more ballast vessels)."*

The laden/ballast ratio has increased, but the Ultramax market has flattened. Fundamentals have weakened slightly. A minor down-move can be expected in the near-term. 

**Top left chart:** The ratio between Rio-Recalada / Skaw-Passero (HS3) and SEA / Singapore-Japan (HS5) is usually a good predictor of tops and bottoms of the HS3 route. A higher ratio suggests a market top, and a lower ratio suggests a market bottom. 
**Top right chart:** Through week 20, Handysize shipment volumes were 4.1% higher year on year, decreasing a bit over the last few weeks. 
**Bottom left chart:** The correlation between the S11TC and HS7TC is over 90%. The correlation is even higher when the S11TC index is shifted forward a few days, which means that movements in the S11TC index often lead the movements of the HS7TC. 
**Bottom right chart:** The laden/ballast ratio is an accurate measure of underlying fundamentals, shown by the high correlation it has with to HS7TC index.
