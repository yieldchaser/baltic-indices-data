## Panamax/Kamsarmax 

A couple of months ago, we listed a few seasonal factors that could result in a September/October rally. However, all these factors are weaker than usual. 

- India coal imports: Longer monsoon than usual, sudden weakening of macroeconomic conditions, including steel demand. 
- US grain exports: Volumes are higher than last year, but more than usual have been shipped on the West Coast compared to the Gulf. 
- Brazilian Corn exports: Lower than forecasted by the IGC and USDA, also lower than last year. 
- Macro: Sudden worsening of European and US manufacturing. 

A seasonal increase in coal winter demand is the main factor we see now that could cause a rebound.

## Supramax/Ultramax 

Last week, we wrote the following:

*The headline market fundamentals remain firmly positive, and the market is higher than in the same period last year, but only just so. 

- The weaknesses in the Panamax / Kamsarmax segment are suppressing this segment.
- The prices of industrial metals are not suggesting imminent improvement (nor imminent weakening). 
- Direction-wise, seasonality has been followed closely all year, although levels have been lower than what was suggested in the last two months. Seasonality suggests this week and the next could be an uptrend, before another downturn until mid/late November.* 

Most likely the market will continue trending sideways to slightly downwards through the remainder of this year. A seasonal increase in coal demand could cause a bounce before year-end.
