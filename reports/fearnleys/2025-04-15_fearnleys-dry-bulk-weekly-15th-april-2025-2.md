## Capesize/Newcastlemax

Imported iron ore consumption in China has continued to rise. This suggests the market could muster a rally over the next month or so. 
However, other indicators look a bit more bearish, like the ballaster/laden vessel ratio and the rising share of the VLOC fleet heading for Brazil.

## Panamax/Kamsarmax 

Since last week, the number of vessels heading to ECSAM has ticked down, so we are now in "overtime" in terms of when the second wave of the ECSAM season should begin. China will obviously be incentivized to buy SAM beans rather than US beans. 
Coal still looks weak, and macro economic conditions are not supportive of a fundamental improvement any time soon.

## Supramax/Ultramax 

Last week, we wrote the following: *The vertical down move in commodity prices and stock markets tells you everything about how global markets assess Trump's Liberation Day policies. The outlook will remain bearish until there is a change to the ongoing turmoil.  
The copper price lead shown below has taken a turn for the worse, and is telling us that the market will weaken during the second half of this year.*

A positive near-term outlook for ECSAM grain shipments will likely help to stabilize the market. No economic data have been released for April, so there is not yet much visibility in this regard.
