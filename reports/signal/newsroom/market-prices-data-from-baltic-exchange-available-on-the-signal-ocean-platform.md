# Baltic Exchange Market Prices data on Signal Ocean Platform

**Date**: February 8, 2023 | **Category**: Market Insights | **Section**: Newsroom | **Source**: [https://www.thesignalgroup.com/newsroom/market-prices-data-from-baltic-exchange-available-on-the-signal-ocean-platform](https://www.thesignalgroup.com/newsroom/market-prices-data-from-baltic-exchange-available-on-the-signal-ocean-platform)

---

Signal Ocean and theBaltic Exchangehave teamed up to bring you some exciting news! We are working together to give Signal Ocean users access to the Baltic Exchange's market price information. This includes all the latest spot and forward prices for dry bulk and tanker markets, so you'll have the edge you need in the shipping world.

Closely following developments and formulating views on the world’s freight markets is often hard; Signal Ocean users can now take advantage of one of the richest combinations of data-driven, model-based, and assessment-based price information to help them make better informed decisions faster. This data can help users interested in predicting market movements to look for correlation between market developments and supply-demand balances, congestion, speeds, ton-miles, and more.

"Baltic Exchange data is best in class and delivers an accurate and independent view of the spot freight markets and forward prices. Our information is derived from independent shipbrokers and is used to benchmark commercial activities and settle contracts in the tanker, dry bulk and gas markets.  We know that Signal shares the same ethos and drive in providing high quality, unbiased market insight." said Mark Jackson, Baltic Exchange CEO.

"The Baltic is committed to ensuring that our data is available to Baltic data licence subscribers on the platforms they use and in the formats they require."


## The available data

Market prices for Dry

- Baltic Prices: Spot/Freight and FFA prices
- Signal Assessment for Spot/Freight

Market Prices for Tanker

- Baltic Prices: Spot/Freight and FFA prices
- Signal Assessment for Spot/Freight
