# Drewry launches OpCost IQ: All-new vessel operating cost benchmarking tool

*04 Aug 2026*

London, UK, 04 August 2026: Drewry, the maritime industry’s leading independent research and advisory firm, today announced the launch ofOpCost IQ, a new vessel operating cost benchmarking tool delivered viaDrewry OnDemand. The platform transforms how maritime organisations benchmark vessel performance by combining Drewry’s trusted operating cost methodology with AIS-enabled operational analytics.

The shipping industry faces mounting pressure on operating margins as costs rise across crewing, maintenance, insurance, spares, lubricants and technical services. Simultaneously, increasingly diverse vessel operating profiles create complexity in understanding cost performance.

"OpCost IQ helps solve a fundamental problem,"said Dr Ferenc Pasztor, Head of Ports and Specialised Shipping Research."Where traditional benchmarking focuses solely on cost comparisons, our new tool combines cost performance with operational context, helping decision-makers identify and understand potential cost irregularities and inefficiencies across their operations."
