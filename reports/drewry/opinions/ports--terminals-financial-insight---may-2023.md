# Ports & Terminals Financial Insight - May 2023

*31 May 2023*

After posting a robust increase of 19.8% in 4Q22, the Drewry Port Equity Index’s performance remained muted YTD (ending 30 May 2023) with a valuation loss of 2.1%. Increased fear of recession, higher inflation and heightened interest rates took a toll on investor sentiment. At the current level, the industry’s valuation multiple (EV/EBITDA) remained in an undervalued zone when compared with its long-term average.
