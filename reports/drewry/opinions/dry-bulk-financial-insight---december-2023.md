# Dry Bulk Financial Insight - December 2023

*01 Dec 2023*

Dry bulk shipping companies reported weak 3Q23 financial results. Despite weak results, the Drewry Dry Bulk Equity Index rose 2.2% in November due to a rise in spot TCE rates. Read on for detailed commentary on the aforementioned topics and the factors driving them.
