# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 15 July 2025

*15 Jul 2025*

For the week ending 11 July 2025, all Drewry Equity Indices posted gains except for the Container and LNG Equity Indices, which declined 0.3% and 0.9% WoW, respectively (vs S&P 500 down 0.3% and Russell 2000 fell 0.6%). The LPG sector led weekly performance with a 7.1% gain, driven by high VLGC rates, followed by the Product Tanker sector, which rose 5.5% amid rising tensions in the Red Sea.On a YTD basis, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index remains the top performer, up 17.5%, followed by the LPG Shipping Equity Index with a 15.4% gain. The only YTD decline is seen in the Drewry Product Tanker Equity Index, which is down 4.1%.
