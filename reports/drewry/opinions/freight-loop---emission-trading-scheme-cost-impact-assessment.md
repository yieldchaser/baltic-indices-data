# Freight Loop - Emission Trading Scheme cost impact assessment

*28 Nov 2024*

Head of Drewry Supply Chain Advisors, Philip Damas looks at the ramp-up in the application of the European Trading Scheme (ETS) at a time when associated charges for shippers and forwarders are amplified by longer voyages resulting from Red Sea diversions.

Drewry believes now is the time for shippers to consider implementing astandard ETS policyfor greater transparency and to help control these costs.

We work with logistics operations and procurement teams in some of the world's top manufacturers and retailers, helping them benchmark their ocean freight costs and improve their ocean freight tender processes by combining robust market intelligence with powerful procurement technologies and proven best practices.
