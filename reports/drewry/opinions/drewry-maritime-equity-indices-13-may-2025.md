# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 13 May 2025

*13 May 2025*

For the week ending 09 May 2025, Drewry Equity Indices exhibited mixed performance (vs S&P 500 down 0.5% and Russell 2000 up 0.1%).

The port sector led the gains with a 4.8% increase, continuing its recovery trend. The product sector also saw a 2.8% increase, supported by newly imposed sanctions from the UK and US government targeting tankers and teapot refineries. In contrast, the Drewry Dry Bulk Equity Index slipped 1.2% and the Crude Tanker Equity Index edged down 0.4%.

On a YTD basis, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index leads with a gain of 11.0%, followed by the LNG Shipping Equity Index, up 6.6%. The steepest YTD decline was seen in the Drewry Product Tanker Equity Index, which is down 13.4%.
