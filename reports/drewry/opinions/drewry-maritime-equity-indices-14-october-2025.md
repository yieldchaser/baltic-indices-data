# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 14 October 2025

*14 Oct 2025*

For the week ending 10 October 2025, all Drewry Equity Indices posted declines except for the Port sector, which rose 1.6% due to a 6.0% WoW rise in AD Port’s share price (vs. the S&P 500 slipped 2.4% and the Russell 2000 dropped 3.3%). The steepest decline was reported in the LPG shipping equity index, which fell 7.9% due to the fall in VLGC spot rates. This was followed by the Product sector, which fell 5.9% as spot rates fell across all product vessels.

On a YTD basis, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index is the top performer, up 37.3%, followed by the Dry Bulk Equity Index with a 23.5% gain.
