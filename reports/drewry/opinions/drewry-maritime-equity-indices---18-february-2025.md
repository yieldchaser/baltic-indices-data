# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 18 February 2025

*18 Feb 2025*

For the week ending 14 February 2025, most of the Drewry equity indices fell, except the Container Shipping Index, which surged 4.2% WoW. This rise was driven by Maersk and Hapag-Lloyd, which saw weekly gains of 8.9% and 5.5%, respectively.

A major contraction was recorded in the Product Tanker Equity Index, which fell 5.2%, followed by the Crude Tankers Equity Index and the LPG Index, which slid 2.5% and 2.1%, respectively.

Meanwhile, the Ports and LNG indices contracted 1.3% and 0.7% WoW, respectively.
