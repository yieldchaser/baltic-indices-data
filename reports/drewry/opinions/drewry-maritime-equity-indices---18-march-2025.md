# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 18 March 2025

*18 Mar 2025*

For the week ending 14 March, Drewry Equity Indices were on a decline apart from LNG, which rose 1.8% (vs S&P down 2.3% and Russell down 1.5%).

Crude declined 3.1% due to a fall in the share price of Frontline Shipping (-6.9%). Similarly, Dry Bulk declined 2.7% due to a steep decline in the share price of Golden Ocean (-12.1%). Meanwhile, Product Tanker and Containers declined 3.1% and 1.2% respectively.
