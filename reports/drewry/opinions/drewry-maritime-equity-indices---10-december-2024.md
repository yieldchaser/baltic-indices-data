# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 10 December 2024

*10 Dec 2024*

For the week ending 6 December 2024, Drewry Equity Indices delivered mixed results.

Port, Container, and LNG Shipping indices gained 3.9%, 2.9%, and 1.5% WoW, respectively, while Dry Bulk, Crude Tanker, LPG, and Product Tanker indices fell between 3% to 6% - WoW. The S&P 500 rose 1% in the same time frame (Russell 2000 down 1.1%). YTD, the LNG index led with a 27.7% gain and is the best-performing equity index in our coverage.
