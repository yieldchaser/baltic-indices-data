# CK Hutchison sells 80% stake in ports business for $22.8bn

*06 Mar 2025*

In a landmark deal in the port sector, CK Hutchison has parted ways with its 80% shareholding in Hutchison Ports Holdings selling it to a consortium led by investment giant BlackRock in conjunction with MSC’s terminal operating arm Terminal Investment Limited (TiL). The transaction is valued at a staggering USD 22.8bn, but what does this deal mean for CK Hutchison, its investors and the industry at large? Let’s break it down.

CK Hutchison Holdings Limited (CKH), founded by businessman Li Ka-shing, is a Hong Kong-based multinational conglomerate with interests spanning retail, telecommunications, infrastructure, ports and finance. Despite contributing only 9% to CKH’s total revenue in LTM 1H24, its ports business — operating under its subsidiary Hutchison Ports — has been a steady cash generator. With an impressive 35% EBITDA margin, this segment significantly outperformed CKH’s overall margin of 23%.

The deal includes the sale of all shares in Hutchison Port Holdings and Hutchison Ports Group Holdings Limited which own interests in terminals in 43 ports located in 23 countries. It excludes Hutchison Ports’ interests in terminals in Mainland China and Hong Kong.

HPHT, a Singapore-listed business trust, owns and operates port assets in South China and Hong Kong. Though closely linked to CKH, HPHT functions independently. Crucially, CKH has clarified that the sale does not include any interest in HPHT or any of its Chinese port assets. While CKH is cashing out on a significant chunk of its global ports business, HPHT remains under its strategic oversight through a management contract, despite its minority stake.

Notably, Hutchison Port separated 90% of its stake in the Panama Ports Company from the rest of the deal. These terminals have become a ‘political hot potato’ since Donald Trump re-entered the White House in January 2025, and the Panamanian Supreme Court is now considering whether the concessions are constitutional. The separation therefore de-risks the remainder of the deal from any delays caused by this pending court case.

Investors wasted no time in responding to the news. CK Hutchison’s American Depositary Receipts (ADRs) soared 17% following the announcement, signalling strong approval from the market. Meanwhile, HPHT’s stock saw a more tempered reaction, as its operational structure remains unaffected by the deal.

This deal is more than just numbers – it is a strategic pivot for CK Hutchison. By unlocking billions in cash, the conglomerate gains financial flexibility to invest in other high-growth areas or return value to shareholders.

For BlackRock, the acquisition marks a further push into the ports and infrastructure sector, reinforcing its commitment to long-term asset investments. Blackrock’s acquisition of Global Infrastructure Partners (GIP) in October 2024 provided it with an indirect stake in TiL, which is owned by MSC (70%), GIP (20%) and the Singaporean wealth fund GIC (10%), and this deal will skyrocket the MSC-led terminal operating group to the top of the global terminal operators league table – overtaking industry heavyweights including PSA International, DP World and APM Terminals.
