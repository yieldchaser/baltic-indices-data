# Drewry launches interactive version of Container Capacity Insight through Container Freight Portal

*20 Feb 2024*

London, UK, 20th February 2024 – Drewry Supply Chain Advisors, the ocean freight cost benchmarking and procurement support division of Drewry Shipping Consultants, is pleased to announce the launch of an interactive, online version of itsContainer Capacity Insight(CCI) service to subscribers via Drewry Container Freight Portal.

"We are excited to announce the addition of Container Capacity Insight to our Container Freight Portal," said Philip Damas, Managing Director of Drewry and Head of Drewry Supply Chain Advisors. "By combining essential capacity and operational insights with our popular ocean container spot market freight rate service, Container Freight Rate Insight, wedemonstrate our commitment to providing those that need to closely monitor industry developments with the tools to quickly interact with, visualise and interpret both freight rate intelligence and key market and operational metrics."

Updated weekly,Container Capacity Insightprovides a holistic view of developments across the main container shipping trade routes. As well as access to the new online service, subscribers receive weekly PDF reports providing key data points and market commentary.

Container Freight Portal is a fully integrated platform, supporting a community of over 500 shippers and senior container shipping stakeholders. As well as providing logistics operations teams and procurement professionals within these organisations with robust ocean freight cost benchmarks it delivers critical market and operational insights, such as; cancelled and scheduled sailings, effective capacity, transit times, dwell time and other key trade lane dynamics, that help informprocurement and service provider management decisions.

Container Freight Portal combines all Drewry's ocean freight cost comparison services in a single platform;Benchmarking Club(S-BMC),Container Freight Rate Insight(CFRI) andDrewry World Container Index(WCI) - widely regarded as the bellwether reference for ocean freight rates.
