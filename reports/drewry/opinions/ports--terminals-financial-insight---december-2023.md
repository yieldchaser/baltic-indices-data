# Ports & Terminals Financial Insight - December 2023

*22 Dec 2023*

Up to the 18 December 2023, Drewry’s Port Equity Index increased by 1.6% YTD, significantly trailing the S&P’s 23.5% surge. Yet, for the QTD, it grew by 8.0%, aligning somewhat with the S&P’s 10.6% rise. Despite this surge, the sector’s EV/EBITDA valuation persists below its historical average. Therefore, we recommend that investors should identify specific opportunities rather than taking a broad sectorial approach.
