# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 20 January 2026

*20 Jan 2026*

For the week ending 16 January 2026, bulk shipping equity indices posted gains, while infrastructure-related stocks declined, except for the logistics sector (vs S&P 500 down 0.4% and Russell 2000 up 2.0%).

Within infrastructure, the Drewry Logistics Equity Index rose 2.9%, supported by higher air freight volumes ahead of the Chinese Lunar New Year. The Drewry Container Equity Index recorded the highest decline, falling 2.6% due to softening freight rates across major routes, followed by the Port Equity Index, which edged down 0.1%. In contrast, within bulk shipping, all indices trended higher, led by the Drewry LPG Shipping Equity Index, which gained 5.5%, driven by a 9.6% rise in BW LPG’s share price. This was followed by the Drewry Product Tanker Equity Index, which increased 3.7% on the back of higher charter rates.

On a YTD basis, infra-focused indices are broadly delivering positive returns, except for the Container sector (Logistics +9.2%, Ports +4.3%, Container -1.7%). Within bulk shipping, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index remains the top performer, up 17.6%, outperforming other segments (LPG: +10.9%, Product: +13.1%, Dry Bulk: +9.6%, LNG: 2.5%).
