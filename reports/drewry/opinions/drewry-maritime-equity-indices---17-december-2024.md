# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 17 December 2024

*17 Dec 2024*

For the week ending 13 December, Drewry Equity indices were on a decline (vs S&P 500 down 0.6% and Russell 2000 declining 2.6%) except LNG shipping (up 1.3%) and Port Index (up 0.1%).

LNG Shipping increased 1.3% as all LNG stocks were up. Crude Tanker and Dry Bulk Index fell 6.7% and 5.1% respectively due to a fall in the TCE rates. Meanwhile, the Container Index was flat (-0.3%) as container shipping equities reported mixed trends.
