# Drewry expands OnDemand to deliver comprehensive maritime intelligence across all major shipping sectors

*03 Aug 2026*

London, UK, 03 August 2026: Drewry is pleased to announce the expansion ofOnDemand, its Maritime Research Knowledge Centre, which now provides comprehensive coverage across all the main shipping sectors, bringing together expert analysis, real-time market insight and proprietary forecasts within a single platform.

The latest enhancements include the addition ofMultipurpose(forMultipurpose Forecastersubscribers) andContainer Ports(forPorts and Terminals Insightsubscribers) sectors - and the introduction ofOpCost IQ, a new vesseloperating cost benchmarkingtool (exclusive toShip Operating Costs’SOC’ clients).

Further to these developments, the platform includes a dedicatedMaritime Equity Researchsection, providing independentinvestment researchon publicly listed companies operating in the industry.

Together, these additions establish OnDemand as the primary destination for all Drewry’s maritime market insight, analysis and forecasts, reflecting the company’s commitment to helping senior stakeholders in the maritime and shipping industry make better-informed commercial, operational and investment decisions.

Alongside the expanded research offering, OnDemand has been redesigned with a cleaner, more intuitive interface, making it easier for users to navigate between sectors and access the market insight most relevant to their business.
