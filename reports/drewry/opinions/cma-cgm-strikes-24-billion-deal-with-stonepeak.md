# CMA CGM strikes $2.4 billion deal with Stonepeak

*02 Feb 2026*

CMA CGM and Stonepeak have announced the creation of a new joint venture—United Ports LLC—which will acquire CMA CGM’s stakes in 10 terminal concessions currently managed by CMA Terminals. Stonepeak is investing $2.4 billion in return for a 25% stake in the JV, which will operate in six countries.

CMA CGM’s terminal portfolio is currently split between CMA Terminals, which is wholly owned by CMA CGM, and Terminal Link, a 51%/49% JV between CMA CGM and China Merchants Port Holdings.

In 2024, the wholly owned CMA Terminal portfolio operated 34 terminals with a combined capacity of 26 mteu and gross throughput of 18.6 mteu/equity-adjusted throughput of 10.8 mteu.

Source:Drewry Maritime Research

The revised structure will see CMA Terminals retain ownership of 23 terminals in 19 countries with a combined capacity of 11.5 mteu. Many of these are small- to medium-sized terminals located in emerging markets, well outside of the comfort zone of most infrastructure funds.

In contrast, the new United Ports portfolio will operate 13 terminals in six countries with a combined operating capacity of 17.8 mteu. The deal includes three major terminals in the US (located in Los Angeles and New York) and four in Spain (Algeciras, Bilbao, Seville, Valencia), with the remainder in high-growth emerging markets (i.e. Brazil, India, Taiwan (China), Vietnam). Total capacity of the portfolio is estimated at 20 mteu, but with  confirmed investment plans to expand capacity at Los Angeles, New York, Algeciras, Jawaharlal Nehru Port and Cai Mep, this is set to rise to over 23 mteu by 2030.  The injection of $2.4 billion into the business will certainly make funding these large-scale investments easier for CMA CGM.

Drewry sees parallels between this transaction and the monetisation strategy pursued by DP World in recent years. The UAE operator has formed a long-term partnership with CDPQ which has been extended to cover a greater number of terminals including assets located in Australia, Canada, Chile, Caucedo (Dominican Republic) and most recently Jebel Ali (UAE).

Our assessment of this partnership in our flagship Global Container Terminal Operators report was that “the fund has been able to invest in mature assets with stable cash flows but also continues to benefit from the operational and market expertise of one of the industry’s leading players”. We hold much the same view when we look at this latest tie-up between CMA CGM and Stonepeak.
