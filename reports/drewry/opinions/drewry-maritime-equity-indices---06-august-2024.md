# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 06 August 2024

*06 Aug 2024*

For the week ending 02 August, the majority of the Drewry Equity Indices declined except Container Equity Index, which rose 4.9% WoW as the Israel-Hamas war showed signs of escalating and equity markets expected the disruption to lift container shipping spot rates for longer periods.

LPG, Dry Bulk and LNG indices declined 5.4%, 4.0% and 3.1% respectively, mirroring the broader equity market downturn. Despite the negative trend, these indices outperformed the Russell 2000’s 6.7% plunge.
