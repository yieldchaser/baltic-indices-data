# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 18 November 2025

*18 Nov 2025*

For the week ending 14 November 2025, all Drewry Equity Indices posted gains except for the LPG and Container Shipping Equity Indices , which fell 1.9% and 0.9% WoW, respectively (vs. S&P 500 increased 0.1% and Russell 2000 fell 1.8%). The highest gain was reported in the Crude Tanker Equity Index, which surged 4.9% due to rise in VLCC spot rates. This was followed by the Dry bulk Equity Index, which rose 1.8% due to rise in TCE rates.

On a YTD basis, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index is the top performer, up 58.0%, followed by the Dry Bulk Equity Index with a 32.2% gain.
