# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 14 April 2026

*14 Apr 2026*

For the week ending 10 April 2026, most of Drewry Equity Indices delivered positive returns (vs. the S&P 500 up 3.6% and the Russell 2000 up 4.0%). Infrastructure stocks gained momentum, while bulk shipping indices were largely positive, except for the crude segment.

The Drewry Port Equity Index recorded the strongest performance, rising 5.7%, driven by gains of 10.6% in AD Ports and 7.7% surge in Westports. This was followed by the logistics sector, which increased 4.0%, supported by expectation of normalisation in air freight operations in the Middle East following the US–Iran ceasefire. Within bulk shipping, the Drewry LNG Shipping Equity Index posted the highest increase, up 2.0%, primarily driven by 8.9% rise in Nakilat’s share price. In contrast, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index declined 5.4% due to fall in Suezmax and Aframax spot rates.

On a YTD basis, infra-focused indices are delivering positive returns (Ports +10.0%, Container +5.7%, Logistics 2.9%). Within bulk shipping, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index remains the top performer, up 47.6%, outperforming other segments (Product: +46.8%, LPG: +31.0%, Dry Bulk: +24.3%, LNG: +14.3%).
