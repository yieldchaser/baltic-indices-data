# Ports & Terminals Financial Insight - August 2025

*29 Aug 2025*

The Drewry Port Equity Index surged 13.0% YTD (ending 28 August 2025), outpacing the S&P 500’s 10.5%. After a 1Q25 dip, momentum rebounded in 2Q25 and continued QTD, fuelled by US trade deals with Europe, Vietnam, Japan and South Korea, plus a second tariff extension for China.
