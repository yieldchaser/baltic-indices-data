# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 23 December 2025

*23 Dec 2025*

For the week ending 19 December 2025, Drewry Equity indices posted mixed performance (vs S&P 500 up 0.1% and Russell 2000 down 0.9%). Infrastructure-related stocks, except for those of the logistics sector, were broadly weaker, as were the bulk shipping stocks.

The Drewry Logistics Equity Index led the gains, rising 3.9%, driven by a 6.0% surge in CH Robinson’s share price, whereas the Ports Equity Index and the Container Equity Index declined 4.4% and 1.6%, respectively. Within bulk shipping, the Dry Bulk and Product Tanker equity indices fell 3.2% and 4.0%, respectively, primarily due to a fall in TCE rates.

On a YTD basis, infra-focused indices have delivered positive returns (Logistics +11.8%, Ports +20.0%, Container +4.1%). Within bulk shipping, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index remains the top performer, up 40.5%, outperforming other segments (Dry Bulk: +23.8%, LNG: -0.4%, LPG: +9.1%, Product: +5.1%).
