# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 04 December 2024

*04 Dec 2024*

For the week ending 29 November, Drewry Equity indices were on a decline (vs. S&P 500 up 1.1% and Russell 2000 up 1.2%) except LNG Shipping, which grew 0.8%.

TCE spot rates fell across the shipping sectors due to lower demand, which put downward pressure on shipping equity indices, with Crude Tanker, Dry Bulk and LPG Index declining 10.4%, 7.9% and 5.3% respectively. Similarly, the Container Index declined 3.8% as spot rates softened despite carriers’ attempts to implement General Rate Increases (GRIs) and boost rates.
