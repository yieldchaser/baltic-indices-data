# Ports & Terminals Financial Insight - August 2024

*02 Aug 2024*

The Drewry Port Equity Index continued to rise in 2Q24, gaining 3.1% following a 1.6% increase in 1Q24. Overall, the index was up 5.5% YTD (as of 31 July 2024), significantly trailing the S&P 500’s 15.8% increase. At the current level (EV/EBITDA), the sector is only at a slight discount of 1.5% compared to its long-term (10-year) historical average of 11.0x.
