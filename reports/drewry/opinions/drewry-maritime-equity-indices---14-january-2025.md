# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 14 January 2025

*14 Jan 2025*

For the week ending 10 January, Drewry Equity Indices exhibited mixed performance (vs S&P down 1.9% and Russell 2000 down 1.8%).

Crude and Product Tanker rose 15.3% and 9.1% as there was a significant increase in their TCE rates, mainly driven by expected tightness in the vessel supply due to an increase in the number of US-sanctioned vessels. Similarly, LPG went up 3.5% due to an increase in VLGC spot rates. Meanwhile, the Container index fell 7.6% as the US port strike was averted, suggesting no freight rates surcharges related to this.
