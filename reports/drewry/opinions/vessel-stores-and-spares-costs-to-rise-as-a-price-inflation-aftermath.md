# Vessel stores and spares costs to rise as a price inflation aftermath

*01 Nov 2023*

While stores and spares spending has shown only a limited increase in recent years, we expect the effects of price rises seen in 2021 and 2022 to appear in the short-to-midterm in the form of higher spending levels. Based on these driving factors, Drewry forecasts a steady average YoY rise of 3.2% in in stores and spares costs in the 2024-2028 period.

Ship operating costs have largely been restrained in the 2021-2023 period as owners took advantage of the resumption in trade growth and rising vessel earnings to keep ships in service for longer over the period. However, due to the significant jump in prices of various items and services, we expect costs to grow in our 2024-2028 forecast period steadily, according to Drewry’s recently publishedShip Operating Costs 2023-24 report.

While a high proportion of overall vessel operating cost inflation was driven by lubes and insurance in 2021-22, growth for these categories has since slowed. On the other hand, we expect a stronger contribution from technical categories, such as stores and spares.

However, stores and spares price rises are to hit different stakeholders in different ways. As with almost every purchasing decision, larger owners with greater scale can negotiate better pricing and hedge against inflationary pockets in their vendors’ supply chains. Smaller owners, who act as price takers, are more exposed to upwards pressure on stores and spares pricing than in past years. Cost inflation is also expected to remain uneven with wide regional variations, often being currency dependent.

While risk of a global economic slowdown is clearly on the cards, deflationary price trends in the sector are unlikely, bar in case of a strong global economic downturn. On the other hand, further price increases represent a significant upside risk for the next several years for all technical operating costs, among them stores and spares costs.

Source: Oxford Economics

One of Drewry’s flagship reports,Ship Operating Costs 2023/24provides one of the most complete annual assessments of ship operating costs available in a single source. Operating cost assessments are provided for 47 representative ship types and sizes, spanning the container, dry bulk, oil (crude and products), chemical, LNG, LPG, general cargo, reefer, ro-ro and car carrier sectors.
