# Ports & Terminals Financial Insight - April 2024

*12 Apr 2024*

While the Drewry Port Equity Index trails the S&P 500 on a YTD basis, indices of Global Terminal and Regional Operators showcase notable upward momentum. Monetary policy intricacies, such as Bank of Japan’s rate hike and Latin America’s rate cuts, significantly influence market expectations.

However, container throughput declined in January 2024 (MoM), with decreases in the Middle East and South Asia counterbalancing Oceania’s growth. Furthermore, higher revenue encounters a partial setback due to the sequential increase in industry costs, thereby moderating earnings growth in 4Q23. Despite these minor fluctuations, the industry valuation profile remains largely unchanged.
