# Drewry launches Container Shipping sector via OnDemand platform

*20 Jan 2026*

London, 20 January 2026: Drewry Shipping Consultants, a leading international provider of research and consulting services to the maritime and shipping industry, is pleased to announce the launch of Container Shipping via OnDemand, Drewry’s fully integrated maritime research knowledge centre. With this launch, OnDemand now provides full coverage across all the main shipping sectors.

Access to the new Containers sector is automatically granted to all currentContainer Forecastersubscribers, representing a significant enhancement to this ‘flagship’ research service, which until now has been delivered in PDF and MS Excel format.

The service provides shipping lines, ports, terminal operators, logistics providers and financiers with rapid, tailored insight to support decision-making in an increasingly volatile business environment.

While Drewry’sContainer Forecasterreport delivers comprehensive analysis of capacity, freight rates, trade flows, regulation and fleet developments, Container Shipping via OnDemand enables clients to build their own custom data visualisations and pinpoint specific data sets when market conditions change.

“Container shipping is being reshaped by geopolitical disruption, regulatory pressure, and accelerating fleet and network adjustments,”said Simon Heaney, Senior Manager, Container Research at Drewry.“Our new OnDemand service allows clients to go beyond static reports and engage directly with our analysts for timely, market-specific intelligence.”
