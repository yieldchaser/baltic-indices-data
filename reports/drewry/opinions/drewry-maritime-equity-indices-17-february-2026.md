# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 17 February 2026

*17 Feb 2026*

For the week ending 13 February 2026, Drewry Equity Indices delivered broadly positive returns (vs. the S&P 500 down 1.4% and the Russell 2000 down 0.9%). Infrastructure-related stocks advanced across most segments, except the logistics sector, while the Bulk shipping indices continued their upward momentum.

The Drewry Container Equity Index led the gains, rising 1.5%, driven by positive investor sentiment following positive preliminary 4Q25 results reported by Hapag Lloyd. In contrast, the Drewry Logistics Equity Index declined 4.2% amid investor concerns that AI automation could compress the revenues and margins of traditional logistics business models. Within bulk shipping, Crude Shipping and Product Tanker equity indices rose 5.4% and 5.0%, respectively, due to strong earnings reported by both crude and product tanker companies.

On a YTD basis, infrastructure-focused indices have delivered positive returns (Logistics +9.6%, Ports +0.3%, Container +1.5%). Within bulk shipping, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index is the top performer, up 35.8%, outperforming other segments (Product: +30.0%, Dry Bulk: +26.9%, LPG: +24.8%, LNG: +10.8%).
