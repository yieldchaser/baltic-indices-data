# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 14 July 2026

*14 Jul 2026*

For the week ending 10 July 2026, Drewry Equity Indices delivered mixed returns (vs. the S&P 500 up 1.2% and Russell 2000 up 0.6%). Infrastructure-related indices (Ports, Logistics and Container) posted varied returns, while bulk shipping indices (Dry bulk, Product tanker, Crude tanker, LPG and LNG) showed an upward trend.

The Drewry Port Equity Index increased 5.1% due to a 5.8% rise in GTOs, while the Drewry Logistics Equity Index and Drewry Container Equity Index were broadly flat, moving 0.7% and -0.8%, respectively. Within bulk shipping, the Drewry LPG Shipping Equity Index increased 5.9%, driven by higher freight rates. This was followed by the Drewry Product Tanker Equity Index, which increased 5.0% amid an increase in product trade flows. The Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index gained 3.8% due to higher Suezmax and Aframax spot earnings as tensions in the Middle East escalated.

On a YTD basis, infra-focused indices have delivered mixed returns (Ports +25.5%, Logistics +12.1%, Container -1.0%). Within bulk shipping, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index continues to be the top performer, up 61.9%, outperforming other segments (Product: +42.2%, LPG: +41.7%, Dry Bulk: +28.4%, LNG: +11.1%).
