# Drewry launches Container Shipping Market Signals dashboard

*04 Sep 2026*

London, UK, 04 September 2026: Drewry, the maritime industry’s leading independent research and advisory firm, announces the introduction of a new, monthly-updated dashboard of key container shipping market indicators to help industry stakeholders assess the impact of volatility on their businesses.

Container shipping runs on market signals: Rate volatility. Port congestion. Sailing schedules. Blank sailings. Fuel costs. Capacity swings. Most stakeholders piece together these indicators from scattered sources - an email here, a forwarder note there, a terminal alert somewhere else. By the time you’ve built a coherent picture, the market’s moved.

Accessible via Drewry’s Container Freight Portal, theContainer Shipping Market Signalsdashboard consolidates the metrics that matter - World Container Index, blank sailings, port congestion, global throughput, fuel costs, demand signals, and more - into one monthly view. More importantly, we translate these signals into decisions: what’s changed, why it matters and how to respond, with recommendations tailored to your role - whether shipper, carrier or terminal operator.

This month’s Sept-26 dashboard is open to all World Container Index, Port Throughput and Logistics Executive Briefings subscribers. From next month, access will be restricted to subscribers of Drewry’scontainer shippingservices only.
