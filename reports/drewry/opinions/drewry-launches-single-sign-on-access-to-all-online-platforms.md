# Drewry launches single sign-on access to all online platforms

*25 May 2026*

London, 25 May 2026: Drewry is pleased to announce that secure, single sign-on (SSO) access has now been extended to Container Freight Portal (CFP) subscription services.

With this change, all CFP subscribers mustreset their website passwords- in so doing, validating their email addresses.

The introduction of SSO access, based on a subscriber’s Drewry website login credentials, has long been an ambition and achieving this transition is an important milestone, allowing subscribers to seamlessly transition between website and Drewry’s two flagship online research platforms; CFP andOnDemand.

Please note that the extension of SSO to CFP does not impact website users nor subscribers to Drewry’sOnDemand services.

Any clients requiring assistance with the transition or password reset should contact Drewry’s customer service team atenquiries@drewry.co.uk

DrewryOnDemandis a powerful online platform which transform the way subscribers can explore and interact with Drewry’s Maritime Research services.

Full access is granted toDrewry Forecaster subscribersrepresenting a significant enhancement to these ’flagship’ subscription packages. The platform now covers all the main shipping sectors: Dry Bulk, LNG, LPG, Crude, Product and Chemical Tanker, MPV and now Container shipping.

The platform is fully-integrated, combining fleet, trade, rates and valuation data with our proprietary AIS analytics. Market and fleet data is also updated as it becomes available, complementing our detailed market analysis, forecasts and opinions to provide a complete picture of the current market and its future development.
