# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 15 April 2025

*15 Apr 2025*

Following the US government’s announcement of a three-month pause on tariffs for all countries except China, most of the Drewry Equity Indices experienced significant gains in the week ending 11 April 2025, except the port index.

This upward movement mirrored broader market trends, with the S&P 500 rising 5.7% and the Russell 2000 edging up 1.8% WoW. The Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index led the gains, surging 14.6% WoW, followed by the Drewry Product Tanker Equity Index, which climbed 11.8%. In contrast, the Drewry Port Equity Index declined 2.7% WoW, weighed down by escalating US-China tensions that negatively affected port operators with greater exposure to China.
