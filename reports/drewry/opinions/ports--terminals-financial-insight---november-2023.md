# Ports & Terminals Financial Insight - November 2023

*02 Nov 2023*

As of October 31, 2023, the Drewry Port Equity Index fell by 8.7%, in contrast to the substantial 9.2% increase seen in the S&P. Year-to-date, Global Terminal Operators (GTOs) and Regional Terminal Operators (RTOs) declined by 9.4% and 7.5%, respectively. However, when examining the quarter-to-date performance, the Drewry Port Equity Index decreased by 2.9%, matching the 2.2% decline in the broader S&P. Both GTOs and RTOs continued to show negative growth, with decreases of 2.7% and 3.4%, respectively.
