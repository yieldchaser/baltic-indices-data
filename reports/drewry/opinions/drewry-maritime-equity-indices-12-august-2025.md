# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 12 August 2025

*12 Aug 2025*

For the week ending 08 August 2025, all the Drewry Equity Indices were on an upward trend (vs S&P 500 up 2.4% and Russell 2000 rose 2.4%).

The LPG Shipping Equity Index led the gains, rising 9.4% due to a surge in VLGC spot rates. This was followed by the Port sector, which increased 5.2% as stocks rebounded from prior losses.

On a YTD basis, the Drewry LPG Shipping Equity Index leads with a gain of 24.2%, followed by the Crude Equity Index, up 17.5%. Drewry Product Tanker Equity Index is the only index to have shown a decline YTD, down 0.1%.
