# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 19 August 2025

*19 Aug 2025*

For the week ending 15 August 2025, all Drewry Equity Indices posted declines except for the Port and LNG Shipping Equity Index, which rose 0.3% and 0.2% WoW, respectively (vs S&P 500 increased 0.9% and Russell 2000 rose 3.1%).

The steepest decline was reported by the Dry Bulk sector, which fell 4.2% due to a fall in the stock price of DS Norden. This was followed by the Crude sector, which fell 2.9% due to weak demand signals from the US and China.

On a YTD basis, the Drewry LPG Shipping Equity Index remains the top performer, up 21.5%, followed by the Port Equity Index with a 14.7% gain. The only YTD decline is seen in the Drewry Product Tanker Equity Index, which is down 1.9%.
